# Story

Metropolis is Antwerp as a playground with a pulse. The streets are already there: tram lines, one-way boulevards, shop fascias, the Scheldt in the distance. Walking them is beautiful. Walking them with nothing to do is empty.

The game is the rise of three men the old powers will not promote.

## The city they inherit

Antwerp runs on layers that the news keeps naming without naming the same people twice:

- The **port** is where Europe’s cocaine arrives in containers and bananas. A box is opened by someone who was paid, threatened, or both.
- The **diamond quarter** is where value becomes paper, trust, and a stone that fits in a pocket. Jewish and Indian traders sit across from each other every day. Most of them are legitimate. A few deals are not.
- The **streets** east of the ring run on corners, scooters, and shops that take cash when banks ask too many questions.
- A **grenade at a front door** is how this city says *we know where you sleep* — a language of intimidation aimed at workers, rivals, and anyone who touched the wrong shipment.

This bible treats those patterns as weather. The characters are fictional. The shops they hit are fictional. The worker at the door is fictional.

## Three floors, one war

The old factions each own one floor and spend their lives fighting over the other three.

| Floor | Who thinks they own it | What it actually is |
|-------|------------------------|---------------------|
| Port | Flemish dock families | Boxes in, silence out |
| Street | Arab street crews | Corners, collectors, cash shops |
| Bourse | A discreet Hassidic network | Stones, trust, value that can leave on a plane |

Weapons sit under the table on every floor. Money laundering is the hallway between them: dirty euros become a kebab till, a car invoice, a diamond invoice, a quiet account.

Nobody at the top shares. That is why the empire stays thin and the wars stay loud.

## How the three meet

Pieter drives. Mo collects. Jacob carries a closed parcel and is told not to open it.

They are not friends at first. They are three errands on the same shipment — a box that left the docks, a handoff in Borgerhout, a stone-shaped lie near the bourse. Each of them is useful enough to be used and not trusted enough to be paid like family.

The shipment goes wrong the way shipments go wrong: someone talks, someone gets greedy, the police get close enough that the old men vanish and leave the boys holding the bag. Pieter, Mo, and Jacob look at each other and understand the same thing at the same time.

They were never going to get a cut. They were the cut.

So they keep the partnership quiet. No name on a jacket. A mark on a wall when a street needs to know. They start at the bottom of every trade — tags, corners, collections, small stones, ugly messages — and they aim at an empire that crosses all four trades: **diamonds, drugs, laundry, weapons**.

## Why the partnership works

Each of them can walk into a room the other two cannot.

- Pieter can still sit in a Flemish café on Mechelsesteenweg and hear who is hiring drivers this week.
- Mo can still stand on a Borgerhout corner and know who is bluffing.
- Jacob can still carry a closed envelope through the diamond streets without looking like a tourist.

Together they can move a profit from quay to corner to bourse. Alone, each of them is stuck on his father’s floor.

## Tone

The fantasy is power and loyalty under pressure — GTA with Antwerp’s real geography and Antwerp’s real crime headlines as flavour. The fantasy is not a race war, not a synagogue raid, not shooting civilians for sport.

When the three win, a street knows their mark, a corner is quiet, a till is lighter, a stone has moved, a harbor man has been warned, and the police are looking at empty asphalt. When they lose, heat rises, a faction notices, and the city closes around them the way Antwerp always closes: slowly, then all at once.

Walking is the stage. The jobs are the play.

## Signs

The three keep one mark: three ticks, `viewer/signs/mark-three.svg`. It is paint on a wall, not a wanted star.

Police heat uses a sheriff badge. Each old floor has its own sign, and only that sign: a Star of David for the diamond network, an anchor for the dock families, a crescent for the street crews. The catalog is [signs.md](signs.md). A mission can send a black car flying that sign. Nothing the player does on the pavement picks a clan for them.
