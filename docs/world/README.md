# Metropolis world bible

This folder is the fiction of Metropolis: a GTA-like walk through Antwerp that becomes a story about three young men building an empire from the bottom.

The project [README](../../README.md) is the build manual. These pages are world building only. Nothing here is a feature checklist or an implementation plan.

## The three

The playable cast is already in the viewer: **Pieter**, **Mo**, and **Jacob**.

They have no public name. They do not print a crew on a jacket. On a wall the sign is a **mark** — three short ticks, side by side, the height of a hand. People who notice it only say *"the three."* That is enough.

## The ethic

Metropolis is about crime as trade and territory, not about hate or sport killing.

- A **faction** is a criminal current, not a people. Most Flemish, Arab, and Jewish Antwerp is outside this story. Ordinary families, synagogues, mosques, schools, and sidewalks are the city the three move through — not targets.
- **Indians** in the diamond quarter are counterparties and competitors in trade. They are not a fourth faction page.
- **Violence** is a message inside the business: a corner cleared, a door warned, a chase survived. Random assault on civilians is outside the fiction.
- Pocketing cash off a dead pedestrian (what the viewer can do today) is a sandbox leftover. It is not part of this world bible. The empire is built on diamonds, drugs, laundry, and weapons — not mugging strangers.

Write every new page as if a newspaper reader in Antwerp would recognise the *shape* of the story (the port, the stones, the cash shops, the warning at a door) and still know that the names, the shops, and the boxes are invented.

## Maps of the fiction

Places follow the maps already named in the menu, including tiles that are not open yet:

- **Klein Antwerpen / Harmonie** — the respectable face; cafés, Velo, trams; where the three can still look like neighbours
- **Antwerpen City** — centrum toward the Scheldt; the diamond streets around Pelikaanstraat
- **Borgerhout** — east of the ring; corners and cash shops
- **Harbor** — port and quays; boxes, trucks, and people who open them
