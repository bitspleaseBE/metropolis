# Faction: Flemish (dock families)

A criminal current among Flemish Antwerp — not Flemish Antwerp itself. Most of the city that speaks Dutch, drinks in Harmonie, and works an ordinary job has nothing to do with these people.

Pieter grew up adjacent to them. He is useful. He is not inside.

## Who they are

Dock families and their cousins: truckers, warehouse men, café owners who know which conversations to forget. They think in containers, shift rotas, and who can be leaned on without a scene. Their power smells like diesel and coffee, not cologne.

They prefer problems that look like logistics. A box that never existed. A driver who was never hired. A worker who suddenly understands he should stay home.

## Strengths

- **The port.** Access to quays, trucks, and the people who open boxes. When cocaine or weapons move through Antwerp, this floor is where the silence is bought.
- **Cars and routes.** They know the ring, the one-ways, the industrial estates. Escapes and deliveries are their native language.
- **Weapons as tools.** Guns are inventory, not fashion. Intimidation at a door — including the city’s ugly habit of a warning left on a step — sits closer to their world than to the bourse.
- **Respectable cover.** A Flemish face in a Mechelsesteenweg café does not trip the same alarm a hoodie does on Pelikaanstraat.

## Weaknesses

- **The diamond quarter.** Stones, trust chains, and Indian/Jewish trade talk are a foreign dialect. They can steal a bag. They cannot *price* a stone or sit a deal.
- **Borgerhout corners.** On someone else’s street they are tourists with the wrong accent. Standing over a dealer does not make the corner theirs.
- **Laundry through horeca.** Busy Maghrebi cash shops are not their network. Dirty money that needs a kebab till has to go through someone else.
- **Pride.** They treat other floors as temporary subcontractors. That blindness is why three boys can steal a shipment’s leftovers from under them.

## Relationship to the three

Pieter is the door they left unlocked. They will call him family until he takes a cut they did not offer. Then he is a problem that looks like logistics.

Mo is noise. Jacob is a puzzle they would rather not examine — a coat that means the bourse is involved, and the bourse is a place they resent needing.

## Territory in the fiction

Harbor tiles, industrial edges, Flemish cafés in Klein Antwerpen and the wider city. Their wars spill east when a box’s contents hit the street — and that is where they collide with the Arab floor.
